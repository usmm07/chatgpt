Who we are

About Diio

We are a retail technology team focused on making small-format grocery stores as efficient as large supermarkets.

Our mission

Give cashiers and owners a single, simple toolset to manage checkout, payments, and daily reporting without extra hardware.

Our story

Diio started on the roads between Tashkent and Samarkand, watching how small shops serve travelers. We designed a flexible system that works online or offline with the same interface in Uzbek, Russian, and English.

What we deliver

Solutions for every checkout

Whether you run a neighborhood market or a roadside kiosk, Diio keeps your checkout fast, transparent, and compliant.

Modern POS

Touch-friendly screens, quick item search, barcode scan support, and instant receipt printing or SMS receipts.

Payments

Accept cards, QR wallets, and cash in one flow. Automated cash drawer reports and shift summaries.

Analytics

Daily revenue snapshots, top-selling items, and staff performance insights accessible from any device.
